Passwords and protected pages

AI PagePolish by PageTweaker keeps its own website sessions after you sign in, but it cannot use Chrome or Safari password-manager extensions. Use your password manager’s app or menu to copy and paste credentials. AI PagePolish by PageTweaker never stores them itself.

Open from another app

Install AI PagePolish by PageTweaker in Applications. It registers as a handler for public web links and HTML/web-link files, so apps with an “Open with” or browser picker can offer AI PagePolish by PageTweaker. It never makes itself your default browser automatically.
