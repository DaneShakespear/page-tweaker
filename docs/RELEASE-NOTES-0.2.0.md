# AI PagePolish by PageTweaker v0.2.0

This is the first release under the `AI PagePolish by PageTweaker` name and a substantial expansion of what can be opened and explained.

## What is new

- Paste macOS screenshots directly from the clipboard.
- Drop or choose PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, BMP, and SVG images.
- Open images through Finder, Open With, or the app icon.
- Mark up images with the same drawings, explanations, notes, screenshots, and AI handoff used for web pages.
- Adjust margin and padding independently on every side.
- Keep wide desktop previews at their real width with horizontal scrolling.
- Resize, maximize, or use the app full screen.
- Use the renamed `AI PagePolish by PageTweaker` app while existing `page-tweaker://` shortcuts continue to work.

## Also included

- Clearer startup guidance and more comfortable opening-screen spacing.
- Direct clipboard and image-input guidance in the app and public documentation.
- Browser navigation, responsive preview isolation, grouped markup, live visual adjustments, and draggable AI handoff from v0.1.16.

## Install

Download the Apple Silicon DMG, open it, and drag **AI PagePolish by PageTweaker** into Applications.

The app is ad-hoc signed for bundle integrity. It is not Developer ID signed or Apple notarized. Follow the safe per-app first-launch steps in [Installing AI PagePolish by PageTweaker](INSTALLING.md). Never disable Gatekeeper system-wide.
